Sample Civil Code (Illustrative Demo Text)

This document is an original, fictional sample authored for this project to
illustrate how a general civil code might be structured. It does not
reproduce, paraphrase, or derive from any specific real statute of Pakistan
or any other jurisdiction, and must not be relied on as legal authority or
legal advice.

Section 1. Definitions
In this Code, "person" includes an individual, a partnership, and a body
corporate, unless the context otherwise requires. "Contract" means an
agreement enforceable by law between two or more persons.

Section 2. Capacity to Contract
Every person is competent to contract who is of the age of majority
according to the law to which such person is subject, who is of sound
mind, and who is not disqualified from contracting by any law to which
such person is subject.

Section 3. Free Consent
Consent is said to be free when it is not caused by coercion, undue
influence, fraud, misrepresentation, or mistake, as those terms are
defined elsewhere in this Code. An agreement without free consent is
voidable at the option of the party whose consent was not free.

Section 4. Void Agreements
An agreement made without lawful consideration, or in restraint of a
lawful profession, trade, or business, or the meaning of which is
uncertain, is void to the extent of such uncertainty.

Section 5. Damages for Breach
Where a contract has been broken, the party who suffers loss is entitled
to receive, from the party who has broken the contract, compensation for
loss or damage caused which naturally arose in the usual course of things
from such breach, or which the parties knew, when they made the
contract, to be likely to result from the breach of it.

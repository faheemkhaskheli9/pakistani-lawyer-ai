Sample Criminal Procedure Code (Illustrative Demo Text)

This document is an original, fictional sample authored for this project
to illustrate how a general code of criminal procedure might be structured.
It does not reproduce, paraphrase, or derive from any specific real statute
of Pakistan or any other jurisdiction, and must not be relied on as legal
authority or legal advice.

Section 1. Cognizable and Non-Cognizable Offences
An offence is "cognizable" when a police officer may, in accordance with
the rules made under this Code, arrest a person without a warrant. An
offence is "non-cognizable" when a police officer has no such authority
without the order of a competent court.

Section 2. First Information Report
Every information relating to the commission of a cognizable offence, if
given orally to an officer in charge of a police station, shall be
reduced to writing, read over to the informant, and signed by the person
giving it. A copy shall be given free of cost to the informant.

Section 3. Bail in Non-Cognizable Cases
When a person accused of a non-cognizable offence is arrested without a
warrant, that person shall be entitled to be released on bail, subject to
such conditions as the court may reasonably impose to secure attendance.

Section 4. Powers of Investigation
An officer in charge of a police station may, without the order of a
magistrate, investigate any cognizable case which a court having
jurisdiction over the local area would have power to inquire into or try.

Article 5. Right to Fair Trial
Every accused person is entitled to a fair trial before an independent
and impartial court, to be informed promptly of the charge against them,
and to adequate time and facilities to prepare a defence.
